Look at the trajectories behind the totals

GPT-6.1 Sol with Codex passed all eight cases in both protocols. On Easy Hexagonal MIRROR-042 it used 315 actions and hit no walls with vision. With text it took 8,846 actions and hit 3,823 walls. On Easy Square MIRROR-042, Gemini 3.8 Flash with AGY finished in 9 text rounds with 268 wall hits, compared with 116 visual rounds and 40 wall hits.

The text feedback gives a direct answer about each attempted move, and a long batch can probe several directions before the next planning call. Visual feedback can help the agent avoid some of those probes, but reflections can also make passages ambiguous. Longer image histories and differences in the tested clients and model routes are additional explanations to separate experimentally.
